PLAN SETTINGS

Assumptions

Tithe is a manually entered monthly budget amount, starting at EGP 8,000. It stays at your chosen amount until you change it, regardless of salary.
Salary compounds by the annual percentage from the first raise month (default: 9% every January from 2027). Enter your salary before that first raise as the base; do not replace it with an already-raised salary unless you also move the first raise date forward. Rent uses the same base-and-first-increase approach: EGP 12,000 already includes November 2026; the next increase is November 2027, then every November. The 10% increases in November 2027 and November 2028 are confirmed by Michael; later increases are planning assumptions. Rent is reserved ahead for the month reached by the pay cycle's end. Days 29–31 move to the last day of shorter months.

Private by design: this app stores plan data in this browser only. It does not connect to CIB and never asks for bank credentials.
