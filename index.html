RECOMMENDED BANK SETUP

Make the cheque account boring.

1
Existing CIB account = cheque-clearing account.
Do not use its debit card for normal life once the second account is ready.
2
Second CIB account = spending account.
Food, petrol, subscriptions, shopping and everyday card spending happen there.
3
Apartment money stays in the cheque account.
No “remember to transfer it back” problem. Once money is reserved, treat it as already spent.
Rule: Until a cheque actually clears, the protected amount is not spendable—even if CIB shows it as “available.”
